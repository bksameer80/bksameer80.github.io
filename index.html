Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question below to expand the answer.

What is your typical project timeline?

Most landing pages and marketing websites take 2 to 4 weeks from discovery to final delivery. Full-scale web applications generally range from 6 to 12 weeks depending on technical complexity.

Do you build custom designs or use generic templates?

Every website we construct is designed from scratch in Figma and coded with semantic HTML, CSS, and modern frameworks tailored to your specific brand identity.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and accessible?

Yes. All our projects are built mobile-first, tested across major viewports, and structured in accordance with WCAG 2.1 accessibility guidelines.

Can you handle post-launch hosting and maintenance?

Yes, we offer ongoing maintenance packages covering security updates, SSL monitoring, cloud hosting management, and monthly performance audits.

How do we communicate throughout the build?

We set up a dedicated Slack or Discord channel for quick day-to-day coordination, combined with scheduled weekly sprint demos.
